# Meowtosis

A looping photo-collage animation of real cats: legs that turn into cats, an endless tunnel of yawning mouths, and a purring mandala around a giant amber eye, with real meows.

**Watch it:** https://ar4ebald.github.io/meowtosis/

Three scenes loop forever, and each one ends by falling into a cat's eye:

1. **Nine lives at once** – a ginger cat walks a wall above red rooftops while its legs turn into cats, tabby heads yawn on long necks out of its back, and kittens hang off its tail.
2. **Endless yawn** – a black cat yawning with another black cat yawning in its mouth, and so on down.
3. **Purr mandala** – a mirrored kaleidoscope of cats around a giant amber eye whose pupil breathes with a purr.

Sound is off until you press **Sound**. Space pauses, and the arrow keys change scene. **Cats under cats** sets how many levels deep the legs go.

It is one HTML file drawing to a canvas, with no build step and no dependencies apart from two Google Fonts.
